The underlying issue
Search and AI build their understanding from more than your website

Search engines and AI systems don’t build their understanding of you from your website alone. They rely on information and relationships across your digital ecosystem — your website, profiles, media coverage, trusted third-party sources, knowledge systems and the connections between them.

When that ecosystem is incomplete, inconsistent or unclear, search and AI can connect the wrong information, miss important evidence or fail to understand how your professional identity has changed.

Executive Digital Brand Engineering addresses the underlying problem by engineering the information and relationships those systems rely on.
