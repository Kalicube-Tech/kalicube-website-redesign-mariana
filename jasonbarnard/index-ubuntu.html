In 2015, brand data cost Jason a business partner. It was worth it.

Why Jason Barnard kept collecting brand data after Kalicube’s first CEO left

In 2015, Jason and Kalicube’s first CEO, Hugo Scott, had built two tools: one that tracked and analysed brands in search, and one that mined Search Console for the keywords to track. Hugo was his business and technical partner, one of the smartest people Jason has ever met, and he really knows his SEO.

Hugo wanted to focus on the Search Console tool. It would probably have worked. It just wasn’t where Jason wanted to go. He was focused on brand and how algorithms perceive it: in search then, in the knowledge graph next, and now across LLMs, search, the knowledge graph and assistive engines. He had a gut feeling he was right, but he couldn’t prove it, and nobody believed him, not even his own partner, who thought collecting brand data was a waste of resources.

They went their separate ways, and they’re still great friends. Hugo has done his own thing brilliantly, and Jason is really happy for him. Jason kept the brand tool and kept collecting the data. It became Kalicube Pro. It turned out he was right: Microsoft and the crème de la crème of SEO, Ahrefs, Yoast and Semrush, have all asked Kalicube to help with their brand since.

“I’ve never been interested in a quick buck. How algorithms perceive a brand is my obsession.”
