Google and AI trust the name with the most evidence

Why Google and AI still use your old name

Google and AI aren’t being stubborn. They built their picture of you from years of pages, profiles and articles under your old name, and those pages still agree with each other. Your new name appears in a few places, but nothing connects it to everything you did before. So the machine does the sensible thing: it trusts the version with the most evidence, and that’s the old one.

What we do about it

How we connect your new name to everything you’ve done

We give the machines one clear story that joins both names. It starts on your own website, with a page that states who you are now and who you were, and the structured data that says so in machine language. Then we update and connect your profiles, so each one points to the same person. Finally we make the record easy to check, so anyone writing about you gets the name right. We do the work. You tell us the story and approve it. Then we show you the change in Google and AI, measured against what you asked for.
