WHY YOUR OLD NAME KEEPS APPEARING

Google and AI trust the name with the most evidence.

Google and AI have built their understanding of you from years of websites, profiles, articles, interviews and other sources across the web. Much of that evidence may still use your old name, while the evidence for your new name is newer and the connection between the two isn’t always clear.

Changing your name on your website and social profiles doesn’t automatically change that wider understanding. Until the evidence across the web consistently connects your new name to the person, work and history that already belong to you, Google and AI can continue to treat the old name as the stronger signal.

What we do about it

Make your new name the one Google and AI understand.

Changing your name in a few places isn't enough. Google and AI need to understand that your old name and your new name belong to the same person, and that your new name is the one that represents you now.

Kalicube establishes that connection on your own website first, then aligns the profiles, publishers, directories and other sources that hold your identity and history. The result is one connected record: your new name represents you now, while the work and authority built under your previous name still belong to you.

  3. 03

    Reconnect your history.
