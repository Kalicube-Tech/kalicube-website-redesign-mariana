WHY AI GETS THE DETAILS WRONG

When AI doesn't have the right facts, it fills in the gaps.

Google and AI build their answers from information spread across the web. When that information is missing, outdated or contradictory, the result can be an inaccurate description, the wrong photograph or no useful answer at all.

People researching you may see that version before they ever meet you.
