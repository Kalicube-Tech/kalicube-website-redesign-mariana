WHY AI UNDERSELLS YOUR EXPERIENCE

AI sees only part of what you've accomplished.

Google and AI build their understanding of your expertise from the information they find across the web. If your biggest achievements aren't clearly documented and supported by credible sources, they may never become part of the answers people receive about you.

You might be recognised as a founder but not for the companies you've sold. Known as a specialist but not as an international authority. Credited for your current role while the work that established your reputation remains invisible.

The result is an incomplete professional picture that can influence how investors, clients, journalists and decision-makers perceive you.
