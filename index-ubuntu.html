The gap between who you are and what Google and AI understand about you

Search engines and AI systems build their understanding of your brand from information across your digital ecosystem. When that information is incomplete, inconsistent or unclear, what they understand can differ from who you are, what you do and what you’re known for.

What AI Understands

  • Incomplete information

  • Entity confusion
  • Weak associations
  • Missing evidence
  • Low confidence
