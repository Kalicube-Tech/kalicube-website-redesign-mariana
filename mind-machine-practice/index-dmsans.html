How the three fit together

How everybody comes to us

There are two ways in. People meet Jason or see him somewhere and take a quick look, and their team does the research. Or Joan’s marketing reaches them on social media, in the newsletter or through advertising, and sends them straight to a landing page on kalicube.com for their problem, with no search or AI involved. Every page has to make the next hand-off easy.

Entrepreneur first, always

Both routes end with the company on Kalicube Pro. The difference is who opens the door, and it decides how hard the sale is.

Easy
The entrepreneur tells the CMO. They have seen what Kalicube did for their own name, so they say “this is how it’s going to work”, and it does.
Hard
The CMO asks the entrepreneur. “Maybe we should look after your personal brand” is a much harder conversation to win, so we never lead with it.

Why it blurs
The fix
“Profound tracks. Peec monitors. Otterly measures. Kalicube Pro trains.”

It puts Pro in the tracking-tool category, the one it does not compete in.

Lead with what only Pro is: the platform Jason built, and the strategy a CMO needs above their tools.

“a self-serve platform for agencies and ambitious brands”

“Self-serve” drops the expert support, and the reader it names is not the CMO.

For CMOs and their marketing teams, with expert support: we teach you to fish. Agencies welcome, lower down the page.

“The Kalicube Aletheium Engine … the data-driven brain Jason Barnard built”

A second name for the thing the team should be calling Kalicube Pro.

Pick one. If the Engine stays, present it as the core inside Kalicube Pro, never alongside it.

“How the Kalicube Process Increased Organic Traffic for Merci Facteur by 33%”

A result delivered by Kalicube, the practice, shown as the platform’s own.

Say who delivered it, or move it to kalicube.com.
